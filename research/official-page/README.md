## What they show

- **The fur is bright, saturated and clean**, in every image. Colours sampled from the carousel images (approximate,
  compressed WebP source):

  | character | fur | background it sits on |
  |---|---|---|
  | Iggy | ~#fa7795 (pink) | ~#fae3ec |
  | Felipe | ~#0f9bfc (vivid blue) | ~#d5f0ff |
  | Todd | ~#a5ca12 (lime) | ~#e7f3ba |
  | Alfred | ~#fccb21 (warm yellow) | ~#fcf5ce |
  | Jojo | ~#f61aa6 (hot magenta) | ~#f2dcf9 |

  They are lit high-key and soft, with light, airy fluffy fur, dense short strands and a soft glowing edge. The shadow
  side stays the same hue, just a little deeper, never grey, brown or black. Eyes are small solid black dots.
- **Composition:** a pale tint of the dot's own colour fills the background. The UI sits flat in the middle (a desktop
  window or front-on phones), and the mascot peeks in from a corner, partly cropped by the frame, larger than life next
  to the UI.
- **UI:** outgoing chat bubbles take the dot's colour. Phones are flat and front-on, with a white frame and rounded
  corners, and no 3D perspective.
