# OpenAI "dots": facts for a parody launch video

Researched 2026-09-30, the day after launch. Rollout, pricing and access details may change within days.
The official pages (openai.com/index/introducing-dots, help.openai.com) returned 403 to the fetcher, so the official wording
below comes from press quotes and OpenAI's own X posts. Frame and colour notes come from the two official X videos,
which were downloaded and inspected frame by frame.

## 1. What it is

- **Name:** written lowercase, "dots" (wordmark and OpenAI's X post). One agent is "your dot".
  https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2104984504133918973 (2026-09-29)
- **Announced:** Tue 2026-09-29 at OpenAI DevDay 2026, in Sam Altman's keynote (about 10:03 PT).
  https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/29/openai-devday-2026-live-blog/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/openai-devday-2026-live-updates.html
- **What it does:** always-on personal agents powered by GPT-6 Astra. Each one has its own cloud computer and browser, connects to
  4,000+ apps, works in the background 24/7, and only pings you when something needs your review. It asks permission before
  sensitive actions (changing passwords, deleting data) and learns your preferences over time.
  https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/ ; https://9to5google.com/2026/09/29/openai-dots-agent/ ;
  https://fortune.com/2026/09/29/openai-takes-on-metas-muse-with-new-dot-agents-and-unveils-a-potential-google-workspace-competitor/
- **Where:** inside ChatGPT (you create it in the desktop app or browser and can chat with it on mobile), plus Slack, Microsoft Teams,
  Codex and voice calls. Texting is "coming soon".
  https://9to5google.com/2026/09/29/openai-dots-agent/ ; https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/
- **Who / status:** rolling out to ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium in eligible markets. Enterprise and Edu get it as a beta if an
  admin turns it on. For now each user gets one "primary dot"; teams of dots come later. Not available in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK.
  Fortune (above) ; https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2105105086506840421 (2026-09-30)
- **Rollout update (2026-09-30 02:58 UTC):** live for all Pro500 and Pro200 users, starting on Pro100; Business Premium and Enterprise are a day late.
  https://x.com/embirico/status/2105130033618804779
- **Pricing:** no separate price; it comes with the paid tiers. It launched alongside a new $500/month plan, and the $200 Pro plan's limits went down.
  https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-29/openai-unveils-always-on-ai-agent-dots-new-500-paid-tier ;
  https://artificiallyintimidating.com/p/what-is-chatgpt-dots
- **Competitors it's aimed at:** Meta's Muse agent (mascot "Jolly") and xAI's Grok Bot.
  https://www.axios.com/2026/09/29/openai-dots-ai-assistant-devday ; https://sfstandard.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots/
- **Official URLs:** https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/ ; https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot

## 2. Branding

**Wordmark** (end card of the 30 s teaser, frame at 26.5 s): lowercase "dots" centred on pure white, set in a geometric sans
(it looks like OpenAI Sans, but the font name is unverified). Each letter has its own soft pastel gradient and a blurred glow halo.
Colours sampled from the compressed video (approximate):
- d: pale periwinkle to cyan (#f5f9ff to #86e1f6)
- o: lemon to peach (#efe87d to #f9e4a5)
- t: hot pink / magenta (#f578dc)
- s: mint green (#baebbc)
The next card is the black OpenAI blossom logo on white. In the 2.5 min film, the "dots" wordmark opens over a dark scene in
white with an iridescent rainbow edge glow. I found no separate logo shape beyond the wordmark and the characters (unverified).

**Launch mascots:** plush, fuzzy 3D creatures with dot eyes. Users name their dot and pick its shape.
- Alfred: yellow rounded triangle / gumdrop (sampled #fcdd35), round wire glasses, black bow tie
- Felipe: blue fuzzy cloud blob, black beret
- Todd: green frog, bow tie
- Jojo: hot-pink heart, sunglasses
- Iggy: pink, headphones
Sources: https://artificiallyintimidating.com/p/what-is-chatgpt-dots ; https://sfstandard.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots/ ;
https://www.fastcompany.com/91615068/openai-dots-agent-crew-of-friendly-mascots ; teaser video (group shot at about 23 s).

**UI** (frames from the teaser and the film):
- The phone chat looks like iMessage or WhatsApp: white background, the dot's avatar and a name pill centred at the top, a hamburger menu

- The ChatGPT sidebar gets a "Your dot" entry above Scheduled / Library / Customize / Explore.
- The call screen is a full-bleed field of the character's colour (yellow, pink) with mute, red hang-up and speaker buttons.
- Sample message on screen: "Tomorrow's finance review just got moved to the same time as your daughter's recital..."

## 3. How OpenAI presented it

- **Teaser, 30 s, 1920x1080** ("Your dot is ready to meet you", https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2104980481876070819). Pure black
  background, macro close-ups of fur (blue, the grey beret, pink, green, yellow) lit from behind with a rim light, slow drifting camera.
  Centred white supers land in 3 beats: "Always-on intelligence", "Remarkably capable", "Remarkably cute" (the third line is already a joke).
  Around 20 s it cuts to a white group shot of all five mascots, then the phone chat UI, the "dots" wordmark, the OpenAI logo, and black.
  There are only about 3 cuts in the first 20 s, then a burst of quick cuts between 20 and 24 s: a slow build with a fast payoff. It has
  an audio track; the music is not identified and the mix is loud (peak -1.7 dB).
- **Launch film, 2:28, 1920x1080** (https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2104984504133918973). Live-action, cinematic, warm and dim. A set of
  walnut-panelled rooms on a dark soundstage, with retro control consoles covered in coloured buttons and tall framed screens on easels
  showing giant dot characters (green triangle in sunglasses, pink heart, blue cloud in a beret). People talk to their dots and take calls on
  full-colour screens; one sits on a park bench beside a pink call screen. It ends on a wide shot of all the rooms on the black stage with the
  OpenAI logo. About 64 scene cuts, roughly one every 2.3 s.
- **Keynote:** full DevDay stream with Altman. The live voice demo stalled into dead air and the presenter typed instead
  (https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/29/openai-devday-2026-live-blog/). One X post claims the official stream then cut the audio
  (unverified): https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2104997638462652838. Recap video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GjN3xLDuc8o

## 4. "Dots" motifs for a code-made video (our ideas, not official)

- The wordmark itself: four letters, four pastel gradients and a soft glow. Easy to rebuild in CSS or a shader.
- Fuzzy blobs: a fur shader or noise-displaced spheres with dot eyes, rim-lit on black, like the teaser.
- The three-dot typing indicator as the "dots" pun: the dot is "always working", so the indicator never stops.
- A white group shot, then the wordmark, then the OpenAI blossom end card.
- Chat-bubble UI with the avatar pill at the top; a full-colour call screen.
- The super structure "Always-on X / Remarkably Y / Remarkably Z" as a parody template.
