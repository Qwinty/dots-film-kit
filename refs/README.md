## What each one does

**Muse (Meta), the long form.** The genre's full template in 82 s:
- It opens on the app icon, then kinetic type builds the promise word by word: "Introducing" → "Muse is" → "your personal AI agent", with small emoji-icons sitting inside the line.
- Then a chain of use cases, each on the same beat: a chat bubble, a UI card (inbox, cart, form, flight) and the mascot with a status line ("Waiting for permission", "Signing you up").
- Between the cases comes a title card, "AI that ___ for you", repeated about 6 times, and that repetition is the rhythm.
- The payoff is "Done!" with a check mark, confetti and "Your Muse gets it done", followed by the logo.
- The look: white to pale blue gradient, soft shadows, floating cards with depth blur, and a 3D mascot that is the only character.

**Grok Bot, the micro form.** One idea in 10 s:
- A phone lock screen on a moving green gradient, with notifications stacking up.
- The phone steps back, the line "Grok Bot is now available on Android" types in, and the Android head pops up.
- It cuts to a black dot on white. The dot squashes and bounces, becomes the logo mark, and the wordmark slides in.
- Worth taking: a logo reveal from a single dot, and the whole film on one colour.

**Krea, the medium form.**
- It opens on a giant text cursor, typing "Create a @Commercial of @shoe": the prompt bar is the hero.
- "Thinking" → "Product sheet done" → "Researching trends" → "Creating commercials" run as a status list with ✓, while image grids fill in and reshuffle under the list.
- Then the prompt box appears with a cursor that clicks send, the result arrives, and it ends on the URL "krea.ai/agents-beta".
- The look: white, black type, a lot of air, and photo grids as the only colour.

## Genre codes to parody

- Kinetic type that builds one promise word by word, with a tiny icon inside the line.
- A prompt bar with a blinking cursor, then a send arrow.
- Agent status lines ("Thinking…", "Researching…", "✓ Done").
- Floating white UI cards with soft shadows on a pale gradient, and a shallow depth of field.
- A repeated "AI that ___ for you" card as the rhythm.
- A typing indicator made of three dots ("• • •"). For a product called **Dots** this is the obvious running gag.
- A logo that grows out of a dot, the wordmark, the URL or "available today".
- Music: upbeat, light electronic or pop, with hard cuts on the beat (check the refs' audio before writing the brief).
